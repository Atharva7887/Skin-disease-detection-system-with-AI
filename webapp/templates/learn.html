More about Skin Cancer

How this tool works, the seven lesion types it recognises, and how to protect your skin.

How it works

You upload a photo of a skin lesion. Three neural networks (ConvNeXt-V2, EfficientNetV2 and MaxViT), trained on the public HAM10000 and ISIC 2019 dermoscopy datasets (about 25,000 images), each estimate how closely it resembles seven lesion categories, and their estimates are averaged. A rule-based step then flags results that involve potentially malignant categories or low confidence and recommends clinician review. The optional heatmap shows which image regions most influenced the prediction; it is not an outline of the lesion.

{{ disclaimer }} {{ performance_note }} Accuracy on phone photos and darker skin tones is expected to be lower than on the dermoscopic training data.
