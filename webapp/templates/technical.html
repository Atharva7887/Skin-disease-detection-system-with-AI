What do these mean?

Accuracy: share of images given the correct lesion type. It looks good partly because 60% of images are ordinary moles. Balanced accuracy: the average recall over the seven classes, so rare types count equally. Macro-F1: the average over classes of the harmonic mean of precision and recall; it punishes neglecting rare classes. Melanomas flagged: how often the app's risk flag fires on a real melanoma (the flag is a separate rule on top of the classifier: {{ e.risk_flag.rule }}). On the same images it also flags {{ '%.0f' % (e.risk_flag.nevus_flagged * 100) }}% of ordinary moles.

What the confidence percentage means

The percentage on the results page is calibrated: it was adjusted (one number, fitted on validation images only, never on the test set) so that stated confidence matches how often the model is actually right. Before calibration the models were too timid.

Limitations

  • Trained on dermoscopy images, not phone photos. Results on phone photos are expected to be worse.
  • The training data skews toward lighter skin tones and a few clinics; fairness across skin tones has not been measured.
  • Melanoma is the weak spot: the single most likely answer is melanoma for only about two thirds of true melanomas; the risk flag recovers more, at the cost of false alarms.
  • Probabilities are model scores, not clinical risk. Predictions are not a diagnosis.
  • Figures were generated on {{ card.generated }} from the shipped checkpoints.
